How New Texas works

Three spheres under one arch

New Texas keeps three spheres of life apart: community, business, and government. None of them reaches into another. The arch above them holds them together.

THE ARCHGoldThe currency and the store of value, fully reserved and open to audit.
THE ARCHNatural law and propertyYour property, your privacy, and your own body belong to you.
THE ARCHShared moralityDo no harm, keep your word, and care for the people near you.
The gold

The currency is gold

When the old money failed, most everything failed with it. Here the currency is a trust in gold, fully held in reserve, on one rail that anyone can audit.

Reconciled
  • Full reserveEvery gram on the ledger is a gram in the vault. No lender of last resort.
  • One railOne gold rail carries every exchange. There are no competing coins.
  • BarterAlways allowed, always free, and off the rail.
  • No stakingThe metal is never lent. It stays in the vault.
  • SilverBring your own silver and trade it for ledger tokens at a flat fee.
  • Open ledgerGovernment keeps the ledger. The code is open and verified, so community and business can audit it.
  • Seven yearsNo loan runs longer than seven years, so debt can never run away again. Capital is raised as equity among people who know the work.
The one fee

The rail fee

Under 5% of every payment, in total

Every payment on the rail pays the same fee, from a gallon of milk to a jet engine. With nothing to sort, there is nothing to lobby for. There are no other taxes. Court damage fees also help fund government.
